Recommendations

The timelines as submitted put six Enablement builds into the August and September window, and they are all mine to build. Here is what is currently being asked for, what I recommend changing, and why. The only real change is moving the heavy Tiered FTV build to November and December. Everything else stays exactly as it was scoped.

Initiative or conflictCurrent state (as submitted)Recommended stateReason & conflict resolved
The August and September crunch
  • AI Cert L1 and L2 are both in their build phase
  • Tiered FTV, the SLA Framework, the Product Loop, and SMS are too
  • That is six builds for one person in about six weeks
  • Keep the cert, SLA Framework, Product Loop, and SMS timelines as they are
  • Move only the heavy Tiered FTV build to November and December
  • The window asks one person to build more than is possible at once
  • Moving the one build that can flex clears the crunch without touching any fixed deadline
Tiered FTV Service Model
  • Two eLearnings, a CSM agent, and a table read
  • All of it built before the mid-September go-live
  • Build the table read and reference layer in time for the launch
  • Build the two eLearnings and the agent in November and December
  • The service model is still changing, so building it in November and December gives a better result
  • It also takes the largest build out of the crunch
